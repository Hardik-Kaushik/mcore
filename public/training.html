FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Who are these programs designed for?

Students, recent graduates, early-career professionals and working professionals looking to switch to a modern tech stack. Each track lists prerequisites, but most start from foundational level.

Are sessions live or recorded?

Core sessions are live (with recordings provided). Mentoring, code reviews and capstone project work are always synchronous so feedback is meaningful.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Learners who complete the program, submit capstone work and pass final evaluations receive a verifiable certificate of completion.

Will you help me get placed?

We don't make blanket guarantees, but we do offer portfolio reviews, mock interviews and access to our hiring partner network for learners who meet our performance bar.

Can my college / company partner with you?

Yes. We run tailored campus programs and corporate upskilling engagements. Talk to us about a customised program.
